### Overview
Alice in Borderland is a Japanese sci-fi thriller based on the manga of the same name.
It follows Arisu, a gamer who finds himself transported to a dystopian version of Tokyo, where he 
and others are forced to participate in deadly games to survive.

### Main Characters
Arisu Ryohei – The main protagonist, a smart but disillusioned gamer
Usagi – A skilled and resourceful survivor
Chishiya – A cunning and mysterious strategist
Karube – Arisu’s loyal friend, brave and protective 

### Why I Love It
> “Survival is not just about winning.” – Arisu 

I enjoy *Alice in borderland* because of:
- The high-stakes, intense game scenarios  
- The mix of psychological tension and action  
- The deep exploration of human nature under pressure
